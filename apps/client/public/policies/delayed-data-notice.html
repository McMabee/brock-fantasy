Delayed-data and scoring-corrections policy

Last revised: October 29, 2026
Contact: Brock Fantasy support

Data freshness and completeness

Brock Fantasy uses reviewed rosters, schedules, results, player statistics, historical records, and authorized administrative imports. Authorized administrators review statistics and scoring corrections. Schedule information may be synchronized from approved sources and identified as pending confirmation when review is needed. Scores are not guaranteed live. Updates depend on source publication, provider access, manual review, mapping accuracy, and complete statistics.

A delay may last minutes, hours, or longer. No fixed refresh interval is guaranteed for the application. A screen refresh does not cause an upstream source to publish new data. We keep unresolved or unapproved data unavailable rather than substituting invented figures. An absent statistic must not be assumed to be zero.

Approved sources: The Brock Fantasy data team's supplied rosters, schedules and tracked statistics, with retained Brock website evidence used for corroboration. The operator has confirmed permission for the approved data use; see the data-rights statement. Statistics and scoring corrections are reviewed before publication. The sports-data team check and update records daily, including weekends, aiming to update statistics directly after games finish. Review/source delays can prevent immediate publication; this is an operating target, not guaranteed live scoring.

Interpreting displayed data

Use the following definitions when interpreting game and data status. A screen may use different wording; update times are shown where available.

Condition Meaning
Scheduled A reviewed game time is available; results are not complete.
Provisional Available results or statistics may still be revised.
Final Required inputs have completed the approved finalization process; later verified corrections remain possible.
Incomplete or unavailable A required input is missing, unverified, or affected by an operational issue.
Mapping review A source identity or schedule needs reconciliation before it can be used.

Season projections use the supplied athlete forecast. Next-period projections divide supplied season points by supplied projected games and multiply by scheduled games in the next fantasy period. Periods use your league's scoring calendar and may span more than one week. Missing or conflicting inputs display Unavailable; a known period with no games displays zero. Estimates using unconfirmed fixtures are marked Pending confirmation. Rankings, average draft position, and projections describe the inputs and method available at the time. Limited samples, schedule changes, and later corrections can change them. They are not guarantees of future performance.

Deadlines and corrections

Lineup and roster locks use server-recorded game times and approved league-period rules. Delayed statistics or notifications do not automatically extend deadlines or unlock players. Postponements, cancellations, rescheduling, and outage exceptions must follow published league rules; report a questionable lock to support.

Verified source revisions and authorized administrative corrections can change fantasy points, matchup outcomes, standings, and playoff implications after an earlier display. Corrections are recorded with source/reason information through the audited revision and scoring process rather than an unexplained overwrite of totals.

Varsity postseason contributes to historical statistics. It contributes to a current fantasy matchup only when the game falls inside the approved fantasy calendar; games after its final period are historical-only. Historical snapshots retain their supplied coverage and values. Fantasy calculations/display use decimals.

A goalie win is credited to the goalie on the ice during the game-winning goal. For a shootout, the winning goalie receives W and the losing goalie receives L. A goalie must play the whole game alone and allow no goals to receive the fantasy shutout (SO) bonus. Going to a shootout does not award shutout credit. Appearances shared with another goalie receive no SO. Brief empty-net intervals do not disqualify the only goalie used.

Scoring requests remain open until closed by admins. A published playoff advancement requires explicit audited adjudication before a correction changes it. We aim to notify affected leagues of material changes and explain the basis. A "final" result is not a promise that no subsequent verified correction can occur.

Reporting an issue

Send support the league, game/player, disputed value, time observed, and an official source link. We review the source, mapping, rule version, and relevant revision. A report does not authorize an immediate score change, and no correction is guaranteed before your next deadline. See the support policy for response targets and the availability notice for interruption handling.
