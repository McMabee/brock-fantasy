Data freshness and completeness

Brock Fantasy uses reviewed rosters, schedules, results, player statistics, historical records, and authorized administrative imports. The operator has selected manual updates through authorized administrators while sports API access is unavailable. Scores are not guaranteed live. Updates depend on source publication, provider access, manual review, mapping accuracy, and complete statistics.

A delay may last minutes, hours, or longer. No fixed refresh interval is guaranteed for the beta. A screen refresh does not cause an upstream source to publish new data. We keep unresolved or unapproved data unavailable rather than substituting invented figures. An absent statistic must not be assumed to be zero.

Approved sources: Tarik Merchant and the Brock Fantasy admin team's supplied rosters, schedules and tracked statistics, with retained Brock website evidence used for corroboration. The operator has confirmed beta data-use permission and Tarik's approval; see the data-rights statement. Updates are manual. Tarik and the data-entry team check and update records daily, including weekends, aiming to update statistics directly after games finish. Review/source delays can prevent immediate publication; this is an operating target, not guaranteed live scoring.

Interpreting displayed data

The intended presentation should distinguish the following conditions and show the latest successful update time where available. These are descriptive expectations, not a claim that every screen currently displays these exact labels.

Condition Meaning
Scheduled A reviewed game time is available; results are not complete.
Provisional Available results or statistics may still be revised.
Final Required inputs have completed the approved finalization process; later verified corrections remain possible.
Incomplete or unavailable A required input is missing, unverified, or affected by an operational issue.
Mapping review A source identity or schedule needs reconciliation before it can be used.

Until finished supplied projections arrive, every athlete has the same Pending placeholder and equal ranking priority, with a reproducible random tie-break. Drafts may run during this period. The weekly prediction model is also pending; no numeric forecast is invented. Rankings, average draft position, and any subsequently published projections describe the reviewed inputs and method available at the time. They may cover limited samples and are not guarantees of future performance. An unapproved prediction model must not be presented as an established forecast.

Deadlines and corrections

Lineup and roster locks use server-recorded game times and approved league-period rules. Delayed statistics or notifications do not automatically extend deadlines or unlock players. Postponements, cancellations, rescheduling, and outage exceptions must follow published league rules; report a questionable lock to support.

Verified source revisions and authorized administrative corrections can change fantasy points, matchup outcomes, standings, and playoff implications after an earlier display. Corrections are recorded with source/reason information through the audited revision and scoring process rather than an unexplained overwrite of totals.

Varsity postseason contributes to historical statistics. It contributes to a current fantasy matchup only when the game falls inside the approved fantasy calendar; games after its final period are historical-only. Historical snapshots retain their supplied coverage and values. Fantasy calculations/display use decimals.

A goalie win is credited to the goalie on the ice during the game-winning goal. For a shootout, the winning goalie receives W and the losing goalie receives L. A goalie must play the whole game alone to receive the fantasy SO bonus in every scenario. In an ordinary game, zero goals against is also required. In a shootout, both goalies who played the whole game alone receive SO even if goals were scored before the shootout; appearances shared with another goalie receive no SO. Brief empty-net intervals do not disqualify the only goalie used.

Scoring requests remain open until closed by admins. A published playoff advancement requires explicit audited adjudication before a correction changes it. We aim to notify affected leagues of material changes and explain the basis. A "final" result is not a promise that no subsequent verified correction can occur.

Reporting an issue

Send support the league, game/player, disputed value, time observed, and an official source link. We review the source, mapping, rule version, and relevant revision. A report does not authorize an immediate score change, and no correction is guaranteed before your next deadline. See the support policy for response targets and the availability notice for interruption handling.
