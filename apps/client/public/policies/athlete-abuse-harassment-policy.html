Zero tolerance for abuse and harassment

Brock Fantasy prohibits harassment, abuse, intimidation and hate directed at athletes, coaches, staff, volunteers or any other person associated with a Brock sporting team. This zero-tolerance rule applies to conduct through Brock Fantasy and credible reports of related conduct targeting those people.

Prohibited behaviour includes:

  • Offensive verbal or written comments related to race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religion or athletic performance when those comments are abusive, harassing or hateful.
  • Threats, bullying, stalking, intimidation and unwelcome sexual attention.
  • Repeated unwanted contact, disclosure of private information, encouragement of abuse, and retaliation against someone who reports or witnesses misconduct.

Respectful discussion of a sporting performance, rule or fantasy result must remain focused on the sport and follow the community moderation policy.

Review and enforcement

Authorized moderators review the available evidence. Brock Fantasy may immediately restrict or permanently terminate an account for a violation without a prior warning. Zero tolerance does not make an allegation proof of misconduct; protective action and the final decision are recorded through the moderation process.

Affected users may use the existing independent appeal process. An authorized reviewer who did not approve the original decision reviews the appeal. We may preserve evidence and refer matters to appropriate authorities where lawful. Brock Fantasy cannot impose university, team or legal discipline on another organization's behalf; any further action belongs to the responsible organization or authority.

Responsibility and reporting

Users are responsible for their conduct. Brock Fantasy does not authorize or endorse abuse or harassment. Any limitation of the operator's liability is governed by the Terms, applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law, and does not exclude liability or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

If you experience or witness harassment, report it immediately to our moderation team by emailing bfa.support64@gmail.com. Include relevant league/message references, dates and supporting details without unnecessarily circulating harmful content. In-app chat reports are also available.

Support is not continuously monitored. For imminent danger, contact local emergency services. Report handling and records follow the support policy, privacy policy, retention schedule and community moderation policy.
