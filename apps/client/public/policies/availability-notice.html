Beta service

Brock Fantasy is a free web beta. We aim to support reliable league participation but provide no guaranteed uptime, recovery time, or service-level agreement. Native apps are outside the initial web-beta release; their presence in the codebase does not establish store availability.

Service or features can be interrupted by maintenance, hosting/database outages, high demand, security incidents, network/device problems, or incomplete sports data. Notification delivery is best effort and may be delayed, duplicated, or missed. Check the in-app state and published league deadlines.

Maintenance and incident communication

For disruptive planned maintenance, our proposed target is at least 24 hours' notice through the service or announced channel, where practical. We aim to avoid active drafts and important game windows. Urgent security or integrity work may require immediate interruption.

When an unplanned incident is confirmed, we aim to publish the affected features, known impact, and next update when feasible. We will provide updates as useful information becomes available and a resolution notice for material incidents. A separate status page is not promised until its location and operation are confirmed.

We may pause drafting, transactions, data ingestion, notifications, or access to protect users and competition integrity. Account security and reliable recovery guide those decisions.

League actions during an interruption

A request succeeds only when the server accepts it. An outage, offline screen, missing push notification, or unconfirmed submission does not automatically extend a deadline or reverse an accepted action. Authorized exceptions, draft rescheduling, and league adjudication must follow published rules and be recorded consistently.

Report affected actions with the league, time/time zone, and available evidence. We review the server record and communicate any approved remedy; no particular fantasy result is guaranteed.

Recovery and service changes

We maintain or arrange recovery safeguards appropriate to the service, with actual backup and restore capabilities to be verified before launch. The operator's recovery targets are a maximum of one day of lost data and restoration within one day. These targets need backup/restore rehearsal evidence before launch; they are not an uptime guarantee. Canadian backup storage is preferred, without a strict residency requirement. After recovery, we reconcile affected competition records and reapply deletion restrictions before making restored personal information available.

Sports-data interruptions and corrections follow the delayed-data policy. Support availability follows the support policy.

If the operator discontinues the service, the proposed target is at least 30 calendar days' notice where practical, with instructions for requesting personal information or deleting an account. Legal or security circumstances may require shorter notice. Closure does not justify indefinite retention; the retention schedule still applies.
