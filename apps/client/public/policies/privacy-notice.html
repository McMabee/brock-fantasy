Scope and purposes

Brock Fantasy is a free fantasy-sports service for private leagues involving Brock varsity athletics. This policy covers account holders, visitors, support correspondents, and athlete information used in the service. The named operator is responsible for the personal information it handles.

We use information to authenticate users, operate leagues and league chat, calculate and explain fantasy results, send service communications, respond to requests, investigate abuse, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information, display advertising, or use advertising trackers.

Consent and choices

We explain collection purposes and obtain meaningful consent where required. Information necessary to authenticate you and run the features you request is a condition of using those features. Optional push notifications require device permission and can be disabled in device settings. We currently have no marketing-email program; any future marketing would require a separate appropriate consent process.

You may withdraw consent by contacting us. We will explain any effect on the service and any lawful retention that remains necessary. Acceptance of the Terms does not waive privacy rights or substitute for consent to unrelated purposes.

You may register if you are 18 or turn 18 by December 31 of your registration year. We ask for an eligibility attestation, not a full birth date. A participant who has not yet reached their 18th birthday is still a minor under Ontario law; service eligibility does not change that legal status. We explain our practices in plain language and consider a young person's ability to provide meaningful consent. If we learn that an account violates the eligibility policy, we will review it and remove information where appropriate. See Ontario's Age of Majority and Accountability Act and the OPC's consent guidance.

Visibility and disclosures

Relevant league members can see your display name, fantasy team and competition activity, and league chat. Email addresses and credentials are not intended to be shared with league members. Administrators and authorized support personnel may access information needed for their duties. A private league cannot prevent participants from copying content; do not share invite codes or another person's private information without permission.

We use Supabase for authentication and database services and Vercel for web hosting. Tarik Merchant is the privacy owner; Ty Mabee is the operator and support/security owner. The intended production site is https://brockfantasy.ca. Hosting, transactional email, monitoring, backups, and any native push providers process information needed to deliver their services. We limit provider access through appropriate contractual and technical controls and remain accountable for our handling of information.

Cookies and device storage

We use browser cookies for login, account recovery, request security, and your light/dark preference. Current expiry periods appear in the retention schedule. Clearing or blocking necessary cookies can prevent sign-in; clearing the theme cookie resets that preference. Supported native clients store session information on the device, which you can clear through the app/device controls. We do not use these mechanisms for behavioural advertising.

Retention, deletion, and protection

Our proposed periods are in the data retention schedule. Account deletion removes the authentication identity and related personal account records on successful completion. Some league history, chat text, and restricted audit records can remain; removing an account reference alone does not make them anonymous. See the account-deletion policy for residual content, exceptions, and backup handling.

We use access restrictions, protected communications, authentication controls, and audit trails appropriate to the information and risk. No system is completely secure. We assess suspected breaches, take containment measures, and notify affected people and regulators when applicable law requires.

Access, correction, and complaints

Contact the privacy contact to request access to your information, an explanation of its use/disclosure, correction, deletion, or an accessible format. We verify identity proportionately and protect other people's information. We aim to provide access at no charge; any permitted fee would be explained in advance.

Where PIPEDA applies, access requests receive a substantive response within 30 calendar days of receipt. We will explain a permitted extension, refusal, and available complaint rights as required. The deadline concerns access requests and is not a universal deletion deadline. See the OPC's access-request guidance.

You may raise concerns with us or the relevant privacy regulator, including the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, where applicable.

Changes

We will publish a revised date and notify users of material changes through the service or email. We will obtain fresh consent where a new purpose requires it. Contact the privacy contact with questions.
