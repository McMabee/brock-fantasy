We use necessary cookies and, only with your permission, optional appearance storage on the fantasy app and association site. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, sell personal information, or profile visitors.

Your choices

Necessary only and Allow preferences have equal prominence. You can keep using the sites without accepting optional storage. Theme changes work in the current page without saving a cookie. Cookie settings are available in the footer; withdrawing permission removes the optional theme cookie.

Cookies are host-only. Choices on one hostname do not identify or track you across the other site. Local development uses unprefixed session cookie names. Necessary cookies are used when their related function is requested.

External services and device storage

Supabase, Vercel, configured email and security verification providers, and approved athlete-image hosts can process necessary technical information such as IP addresses. This is described in the privacy notice; a browser-cookie choice does not disable essential security. Cloudflare Turnstile verification, when enabled, must be checked for its actual provider storage and accessibility behavior. No analytics or advertising category is enabled.

The web app does not store authentication tokens in localStorage or sessionStorage. Supported native clients have separate device storage; this browser policy does not change their device controls. Clearing necessary cookies can end your session. Clearing the choice cookie will ask you to choose again.

Contact

The app uses tab-limited sessionStorage for unsaved lineup assignments (bf-lineup keys scoped to the signed-in user and team) so requested edits survive a reload, and brock-turn keys to avoid repeating an enabled live-draft sound for the same pick. These are functional records, not visitor identifiers, and last until the tab closes or storage is cleared. Turn sound is off until enabled. Native clients use device session storage and a separate theme setting; this browser banner does not authorize native tracking.
