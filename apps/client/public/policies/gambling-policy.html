Recreational fantasy sport

Brock Fantasy is for informational and entertainment purposes only. It does not offer or provide real-money gambling or sports-betting services. There are no wagers, paid entries, betting pools, deposits, withdrawals or cash prizes. Fantasy points and results have no monetary or exchange value.

Prohibited activity

Brock Fantasy prohibits its users from gambling on any Brock sporting event. Users must not place, organize, solicit, facilitate or promote such bets, whether through Brock Fantasy or in related activity outside the platform. Do not use chat, account/team names, invitations or fantasy outcomes to arrange gambling, paid contests or money transfers.

This account-conduct rule does not make Brock Fantasy a gambling regulator or determine whether a person's activity is lawful elsewhere.

Review and enforcement

A user found, after review of available evidence, to have gambled on a Brock sporting event will have their Brock Fantasy account permanently terminated. Protective restrictions may be applied while a report is reviewed, without prior warning where appropriate. A report alone does not establish a violation.

Further disciplinary or legal action, if applicable, is decided by the university, sporting organization or competent authority under its own rules and powers. Brock Fantasy does not promise or impose those outside sanctions.

The community moderation policy provides notice where safe and lawful, decision records and an independent appeal route. Termination alone does not erase records; the account-deletion policy, privacy policy and retention schedule apply.

Responsibility and reporting

Users are responsible for their conduct. Brock Fantasy does not authorize or endorse gambling on Brock sporting events. Any liability limitation is subject to the Terms and applicable law; mandatory rights and non-excludable liabilities remain available.

Report suspected prohibited gambling to both tymabee@proton.me and gt22me@brocku.ca, with relevant references and evidence. Reports follow the support policy.
