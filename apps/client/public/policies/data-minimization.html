We collect and retain information for defined service purposes. We do not sell personal information, run advertisements, use analytics trackers, or profile visitors.

What is needed and why

  • Email, display name, verified authentication, and security records: account access and protection
  • Calendar-year eligibility attestation and policy version: eligibility without collecting a date of birth
  • Varsity participation and programs where applicable: the existing legal-roster and conflict rules
  • League membership, team activity, draft preferences, rosters, chat, and scoring: the fantasy features you request
  • Optional reply details and support messages: responding to requests; a name is optional
  • Limited request/provider logs and abuse-prevention signals: security, delivery, and troubleshooting

We do not require student numbers, payment information, phone numbers, birth dates, or precise device location. Publicly sourced athlete information can still be personal information and needs source rights, correction, withdrawal, and retention controls.

Your control

Optional theme persistence, launch/news email consent, and draft email preferences are separate choices. Rejecting optional cookies does not stop sign-in or league access. Private draft preference lists are not visible to other managers or commissioners. Restricted support access requires a reason and an audit record. Snapshots expire 30 days after completion or cancellation unless a documented dispute hold applies.

Retention and deletion

The retention schedule assigns category-specific limits. Delete earlier when information is unnecessary, preserve documented legal holds, and restrict provider copies and backups. Account deletion does not automatically make retained chat, names, audit payloads, or historical records anonymous.

These are operational requirements; provider settings, expiry-job activation, and approved publication require release evidence. We do not claim that a policy page proves those controls have been verified.
