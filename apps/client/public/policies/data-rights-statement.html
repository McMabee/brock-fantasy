Approved sources and purposes

The operator confirms approval to use the supplied roster, schedule, historical-statistic and provider-source data in Brock Fantasy's 2026–27 web beta at brockfantasy.ca and its beta.brockfantasy.ca staging site. Tarik Merchant and his team collected the rosters and confirmed individual athletes' information and positions. They track the statistics and own their statistical work and supplied calculations. The operator also confirms Brock Athletics permission for institutional data use.

We use these records to display athletes and schedules, explain historical performance, calculate fantasy results and supplied estimates, correct errors, and retain source/revision evidence needed to explain league results. Historical totals, preseason values and projections are identified separately from current-season points. We do not infer zero from unavailable values or create missing historical game logs.

Tarik Merchant and the admin team approve data, mappings and game rules and handle source corrections. Supplied game logic and source-of-truth values are held in logic/. We retain source references and attribute external Brock pages used for corroboration. Third-party content remains subject to its owner's rights.

Brock Fantasy is independent of Brock Athletics, OUA and U SPORTS. Approved information use does not establish organizational affiliation.

Manual updates and providers

Authorized administrators update sports data manually. The beta has no connected sports-provider API. No U SPORTS individual-game dataset is represented as acquired or independently verified. Accessible Brock game records and HockeyDB season summaries are corroboration, with their scope recorded separately. Data can be delayed or corrected; see the delayed-data policy.

Athlete correction and opt-out

Athletes can contact Tarik Merchant, the privacy owner, to correct their information or request that it no longer be used, without opening an account. The operator has selected an athlete opt-out policy. Send requests to both Ty Mabee (tymabee@proton.me) and Tarik Merchant (gt22me@brocku.ca). Before launch, we must verify handling and import suppression so later imports do not restore withdrawn records. We do not represent an athlete's silence as documented explicit consent.

Retention and necessary historical audit records follow the privacy policy, retention schedule, applicable source restrictions and the reviewed request outcome.
