Accessibility statement

Review-ready draft — October 24, 2026
Operator publication and applicability approval are pending

Our target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA for both web properties, while retaining applicable AODA requirements. This statement is not a certification or a claim that every requirement has passed.

Available controls

Keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus, skip navigation, dialog Escape dismissal and focus restoration, descriptive image alternatives where needed, and accessible move controls for draft rankings support access without a mouse. Theme switching works without optional persistence. Browser zoom and reduced-motion preferences remain available.

Verification and known limits

Automated browser checks cover representative public and authenticated workflows, both themes where supported, and phone/desktop layouts. Automated checks cannot establish full WCAG conformance. Representative manual screen-reader testing, third-party verification behavior, all dynamic states, and final hosted acceptance remain release gates until their evidence is recorded.

Help and alternate formats

Email Ty Mabee at tymabee@proton.me. Tell us the page, what prevented access, and the assistance or format you need. A medical diagnosis is not required. Ty owns the request and can involve Tarik internally. We will work with you on an appropriate accessible alternative.

The service is an independent Canadian volunteer project. Its accessibility target is maintained separately from legal applicability; we do not use the AODA employee threshold as a reason to ignore access barriers.
