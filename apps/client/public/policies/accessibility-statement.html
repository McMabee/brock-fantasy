Accessibility statement

Last revised: October 29, 2026
Contact: Brock Fantasy support

Brock Fantasy works toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA across its web properties and considers applicable accessibility obligations separately. This statement describes available features and known limitations; it is not a certification of full conformance.

Available controls

Keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus, skip navigation, dialog Escape dismissal and focus restoration, descriptive image alternatives where needed, and accessible move controls for draft rankings support access without a mouse. Theme switching works without optional persistence. Browser zoom and reduced-motion preferences remain available.

Verification and known limits

Automated browser checks cover representative public and authenticated workflows, supported themes and phone/desktop layouts. Automated results do not establish full WCAG conformance. Some dynamic states and third-party verification steps may still present barriers, and representative screen-reader testing remains incomplete. Contact us if you need assistance or an accessible alternative.

Help and alternate formats

Email Brock Fantasy support at tymabee@proton.me. Tell us the page, what prevented access, and the assistance or format you need. A medical diagnosis is not required. The accessibility contact coordinates the response. We will work with you on an appropriate accessible alternative.

The service is an independent Canadian volunteer project. Its accessibility target is maintained separately from legal applicability; we do not use the AODA employee threshold as a reason to ignore access barriers.
