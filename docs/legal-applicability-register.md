## Operating facts and review boundaries

The operator describes an independent, free Canadian volunteer project with no
advertising, analytics, profiling, sale of information, paid contests or cash prizes
There are no confirmed employees or institutional operators in this assessment
These facts must be reconfirmed before publication and when the scope changes

The eligibility rule allows registration if a person is 18 or turns 18 by December
31 in their registration year
Some eligible people are therefore 17 and remain minors under Ontario law
Keep understandable notices, optional choices separate, no birth-date collection,
and a route to ask questions or withdraw information
The operator must review meaningful consent and enforceability of terms for these
participants rather than describing every eligible participant as an adult
