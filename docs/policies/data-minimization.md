# Only the information needed

**Last revised:** October 29, 2026\
**Contact:** [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:tymabee@proton.me)

Brock Fantasy limits collection and retention to information needed to operate, protect and support the service. We do not sell personal information, display advertisements, use analytics trackers or profile visitors.

## What is needed and why

- Email, display name, verified authentication, and security records: account access and
  protection
- Calendar-year eligibility attestation and policy version: eligibility without collecting a
  date of birth
- Varsity participation and programs where applicable: the existing legal-roster and
  conflict rules
- League membership, team activity, draft preferences, rosters, chat, and scoring: the
  fantasy features you request
- Optional reply details and support messages: responding to requests; a name is optional
- Limited request/provider logs and abuse-prevention signals: security, delivery, and
  troubleshooting

We do not require student numbers, payment information, phone numbers, birth dates, or
precise device location. Publicly sourced athlete information can still be personal
information and needs source rights, correction, withdrawal, and retention controls.

## Your control

Optional theme persistence, launch/news email consent, and draft email preferences are
separate choices. Rejecting optional cookies does not stop sign-in or league access. Private
draft preference lists are not visible to other managers or commissioners. Restricted
support access requires a reason and an audit record. Snapshots expire 30 days after
completion or cancellation unless a documented dispute hold applies.

## Retention and deletion

The [retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html) assigns category-specific
limits. Delete earlier when information is unnecessary, preserve documented legal holds, and
restrict provider copies and backups. Account deletion does not automatically make retained
chat, names, audit payloads, or historical records anonymous.

Provider copies, backups and retained historical content remain subject to purpose-based review and the applicable retention limits.

Email [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:tymabee@proton.me) for access, correction, deletion,
athlete concerns, or accessibility assistance. No
