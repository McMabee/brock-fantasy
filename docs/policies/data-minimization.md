# Data minimization statement

**Last revised:** October 29, 2026\
**Contact:** [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:bfa.support64@gmail.com)

Brock Fantasy uses information for defined account, league, support and security purposes. We aim to collect the least information needed for the features you choose and retain it only for the applicable purpose and retention period.

## Our approach

Collection starts with a specific service purpose. Necessary account and security processing is explained separately from optional preferences and email subscriptions. We do not sell personal information, display advertisements, use advertising or analytics trackers, or profile visitors.

This statement explains how those limits apply across Brock Fantasy. The [privacy policy](privacy-notice.html) describes the full information-handling practices, providers and rights available to you.

## Information needed for your account

- **Email and authentication records** let you verify your identity, sign in, recover access and receive necessary account messages
- **Display name and account identifier** connect your account to the fantasy features you use
- **Eligibility attestation, registration year and policy version** record eligibility without asking for a full birth date
- **Varsity participation and applicable programs** support the existing legal-roster and conflict rules
- **Limited security and request information** supports session protection, abuse prevention and troubleshooting

We do not require a student number, payment details, phone number, full birth date or precise device location to use the service.

## Information created when you play

League memberships, fantasy team names, draft picks, lineups, transactions, chat and scoring records support the competition features you use. Their visibility depends on the feature and your league membership. Avoid including sensitive information in team names, chat or reports.

Your private automatic-draft rankings are visible to you. Commissioners see preparation status rather than your choices. Privileged support access requires an authorized purpose and an audit reason. Private preferences and execution snapshots expire 30 days after draft completion or cancellation unless a documented dispute hold applies.

## Optional choices stay separate

- **Appearance preferences:** optional theme storage requires your preference-cookie choice
- **Launch and news emails:** subscription consent is separate from creating or using an account
- **Draft email preferences:** you can manage the supported optional draft notices separately
- **Device notifications:** a supported native notification feature requires device permission
- **Support correspondence:** provide the details needed to explain your request; a name is optional on the contact form

Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent sign-in or league access. You can change cookie choices through Cookie settings and manage available account preferences in the app. See [cookies and device storage](cookie-policy.html).

## Athlete and sports information

Approved rosters, athlete names, programs, positions, schedules, results and historical statistics support player information and fantasy scoring. Public availability does not make athlete information anonymous or remove the need for appropriate source rights, accuracy, withdrawal and retention controls.

Athletes can raise a correction or withdrawal concern without creating a fantasy account. Import-suppression controls help prevent withdrawn records from being reintroduced. See the [data-rights statement](data-rights-statement.html).

## Access, retention and deletion

Access is limited according to account, league, staff and service-provider responsibilities. Providers process the information needed for their configured services. The [retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html) sets category-specific limits, including the treatment of provider copies, backups and documented holds.

Account deletion removes direct account records through the supported deletion process. Retained chat, fantasy team names, audit payloads and historical competition records can still identify someone; they are not automatically anonymous and require purpose-based review.

Information should not be kept merely because storage is available. When it is no longer needed for its purpose or an applicable documented retention requirement, it is subject to the service's deletion and disposal process.

## Questions and requests

Contact [bfa.support64@gmail.com](mailto:bfa.support64@gmail.com) for access, correction, deletion, athlete concerns or accessibility assistance. Describe the feature or record involved and provide only the details needed to handle the request. Do not send passwords, authenticator codes, diagnoses or unnecessary identity documents.

Read the [support policy](support-policy.html) for request handling and independent review, or the [account deletion notice](account-deletion-notice.html) for the effects of deleting an account.
