## Approved sources and purposes

The operator confirms approval to use the supplied roster, schedule, historical-statistic
and provider-source data in Brock Fantasy's 2026–27 web application at play.brockfantasy.ca.
The authorized sports-data team collected the rosters and confirmed individual athletes'
information and positions. They track the statistics and own their statistical work and
supplied calculations. The operator also confirms Brock Athletics permission for
institutional data use.

We use these records to display athletes and schedules, explain historical performance,
calculate fantasy results and supplied estimates, correct errors, and retain source/revision
evidence needed to explain league results. Historical totals, preseason values and
projections are identified separately from current-season points. We do not infer zero from
unavailable values or create missing historical game logs.

Authorized sports-data administrators approve data, mappings and game rules and handle source
corrections. Reviewed source records and rule versions support corrections and explain published results.
We retain source references and attribute external Brock pages used for corroboration.
Third-party content remains subject to its owner's rights.

Brock Fantasy is independent of Brock Athletics, OUA and U SPORTS. Approved information use
does not establish organizational affiliation.

## Manual updates and providers

Authorized administrators review sports statistics and scoring corrections. Schedule information may be synchronized from approved official sources and marked pending confirmation when review is required. Brock Fantasy does not provide a guaranteed live sports-data feed.

Brock game records and HockeyDB season summaries are used within their recorded corroboration scope. We do not represent an individual-game U SPORTS dataset as acquired or independently verified. See the [data freshness and corrections policy](delayed-data-notice.html).

## Athlete correction and opt-out

Athletes may contact the privacy contact to correct their information or request
that it no longer be used, without opening an account. The operator has selected an athlete
opt-out policy. Send requests to [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:bfa.support64@gmail.com). The review includes import suppression to prevent later imports from restoring withdrawn records. We do not represent an athlete's silence as
documented explicit consent.

Retention and necessary historical audit records follow the
[privacy policy](privacy-notice.html),
[retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html), applicable source
restrictions and the reviewed request outcome.

## Approval record

The operator retains approval provenance and source references for authorized data use. Source permission does not establish organizational affiliation or an individual athlete’s explicit consent. Requests about accuracy, use or withdrawal may be submitted to the privacy contact without creating an account.
