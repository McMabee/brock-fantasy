# Availability notice

**Last revised:** October 29, 2026\
**Contact:** [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:bfa.support64@gmail.com)

## Service availability

Brock Fantasy is a free web application. We aim to support reliable league participation but
provide no guaranteed uptime, recovery time, or service-level agreement. Native apps are outside the initial web release and are not offered through app stores as part of that release.

Service or features can be interrupted by maintenance, hosting/database outages, high
demand, security incidents, network/device problems, or incomplete sports data. Notification
delivery is best effort and may be delayed, duplicated, or missed. Check the in-app state
and published league deadlines.

## Maintenance and incident communication

For disruptive planned maintenance, our target is at least 24 hours' notice through
the service or announced channel, where practical. We aim to avoid active drafts and
important game windows. Urgent security or integrity work may require immediate
interruption.

When an unplanned incident is confirmed, we aim to publish the affected features, known
impact, and next update when feasible. We will provide updates as useful information becomes
available and a resolution notice for material incidents. A separate status page is not
promised until its location and operation are confirmed.

We may pause drafting, transactions, data ingestion, notifications, or access to protect
users and competition integrity. Account security and reliable recovery guide those
decisions.

## League actions during an interruption

A request succeeds only when the server accepts it. An outage, offline screen, missing push
notification, or unconfirmed submission does not automatically extend a deadline or reverse
an accepted action. Authorized exceptions, draft rescheduling, and league adjudication must
follow published rules and be recorded consistently.

Report affected actions with the league, time/time zone, and available evidence. We review
the server record and communicate any approved remedy; no particular fantasy result is
guaranteed.

## Recovery and service changes

We use recovery safeguards appropriate to the service. Our recovery targets are no more than one day of lost data and restoration within one day. These are operational targets, not guarantees of recovery time or uninterrupted availability. Canadian
backup storage is preferred, without a strict residency requirement. After recovery, we
reconcile affected competition records and reapply deletion restrictions before making
restored personal information available.

Sports-data interruptions and corrections follow the
[delayed-data policy](delayed-data-notice.html). Support availability follows the
[support policy](support-policy.html).

If the operator discontinues the service, the target is at least 30 calendar days' notice where practical, with instructions for requesting personal information or deleting an
account. Legal or security circumstances may require shorter notice. Closure does not
justify indefinite retention; the
[retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html) still applies.
