# Gambling prohibition

**Last revised:** October 29, 2026\
**Contact:** [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:tymabee@proton.me)

## Recreational fantasy sport

Brock Fantasy is for informational and entertainment purposes only. It does not offer or
provide real-money gambling or sports-betting services. There are no wagers, paid entries,
betting pools, deposits, withdrawals or cash prizes. Fantasy points and results have no
monetary or exchange value.

## Prohibited activity

Brock Fantasy prohibits its users from gambling on any Brock sporting event. Users must not
place, organize, solicit, facilitate or promote such bets, whether through Brock Fantasy or
in related activity outside the platform. Do not use chat, account/team names, invitations
or fantasy outcomes to arrange gambling, paid contests or money transfers.

This account-conduct rule does not make Brock Fantasy a gambling regulator or determine
whether a person's activity is lawful elsewhere.

## Review and enforcement

A user found, after review of available evidence, to have gambled on a Brock sporting event
will have their Brock Fantasy account permanently terminated. Protective restrictions may be
applied while a report is reviewed, without prior warning where appropriate. A report alone
does not establish a violation.

Further disciplinary or legal action, if applicable, is decided by the university, sporting
organization or competent authority under its own rules and powers. Brock Fantasy does not
promise or impose those outside sanctions.

The [community moderation policy](community-moderation-guidelines.html) provides
notice where safe and lawful, decision records and an independent appeal route. Termination
alone does not erase records; the
[account-deletion policy](account-deletion-notice.html),
[privacy policy](privacy-notice.html) and
[retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html) apply.

## Responsibility and reporting

Users are responsible for their conduct. Brock Fantasy does not authorize or endorse
gambling on Brock sporting events. Any liability limitation is subject to the
[Terms](terms-of-use.html) and applicable law; mandatory rights and
non-excludable liabilities remain available.

Report suspected prohibited gambling to
**tymabee@proton.me**, with relevant references and evidence. Reports follow
the [support policy](support-policy.html).
