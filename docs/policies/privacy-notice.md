# Privacy policy

**Contact:** [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:bfa.support64@gmail.com)

## Scope and purposes

Brock Fantasy is a free fantasy-sports service for private leagues involving Brock varsity
athletics. This policy covers account holders, visitors, support correspondents, and athlete
information used in the service. The named operator is responsible for the personal
information it handles.

We use information to authenticate users, operate leagues and league chat, calculate and
explain fantasy results, send service communications, respond to requests, investigate
abuse, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information, display advertising, or
use advertising trackers.

## Information we handle

### Account and authentication

Email, display name, account ID, verification/recovery status, calendar-year eligibility attestation and policy version, password and optional authenticator information. Supabase Auth processes credentials; passwords are not stored in readable form by Brock Fantasy.

### League activity

Memberships, team names, invitations, draft picks, queues and private automatic-draft preferences, lineups, trades, waivers, results, and related timestamps, to run and audit competition.

### Community information

Chat text, reports, mute settings, and moderation decisions, to provide communication and enforce community rules.

### Technical and security information

IP address or related rate-limit signals, request/device/browser metadata where logged by our providers, session/security cookies, and audit records, to secure and troubleshoot the service.

### Notifications

In-app notices and delivery records; device push tokens and message payloads if you enable a supported native notification feature. Native push is outside the initial web release.

### Support and privacy requests

Contact details, correspondence, evidence you submit, and necessary verification information, to handle the request.

### Athlete and sports information

Athlete names, team/position, jersey numbers, approved roster details, schedules, results, historical statistics, and source revisions, to present player information and calculate fantasy scoring.

We do not request student numbers, payment details, dates of birth, phone numbers, or
precise device location. An optional avatar URL is supported in the data model; if enabled,
it may reveal profile information and cause requests to the image host. Avoid putting
sensitive information in chat, team names, or support attachments.

Athlete information can be personal information even when obtained from a public source. The
operator confirms approved source use and team collection/ownership in the
[data-rights statement](data-rights-statement.html). Athletes may submit correction or opt-out requests to the privacy contact. Withdrawn records are subject to import-suppression controls to prevent their reintroduction. Institutional/source permission does not record an individual athlete’s explicit
consent. Athletes may contact the privacy contact about accuracy or use of their information
without creating an account.

Automatic-draft preferences are visible to their owner. Commissioners see preparation
status, not private lists. Authorized support access requires privileged authentication and
an audit reason. Preferences and execution snapshots expire 30 days after completion or
cancellation unless a documented hold applies.

## Consent and choices

We explain collection purposes and obtain meaningful consent where required. Information
necessary to authenticate you and run the features you request is a condition of using those
features. Optional push notifications require device permission and can be disabled in
device settings. Optional association launch/news consent is separate from account access
and service notices. Marketing messages require approved sender details, appropriate consent
and an account-free unsubscribe route.

You may withdraw consent by contacting us. We will explain any effect on the service and any
lawful retention that remains necessary. Acceptance of the Terms does not waive privacy
rights or substitute for consent to unrelated purposes.

You may register if you are 18 or turn 18 by December 31 of your registration year. We ask
for an eligibility attestation, not a full birth date. A participant who has not yet reached
their 18th birthday is still a minor under Ontario law; service eligibility does not change
that legal status. We explain our practices in plain language and consider a young person's
ability to provide meaningful consent. If we learn that an account violates the eligibility
policy, we will review it and remove information where appropriate. See Ontario's
[Age of Majority and Accountability Act](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90a07)
and the OPC's
[consent guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/p_principle/principles/p_consent/).

## Visibility and disclosures

Relevant league members can see your display name, fantasy team and competition activity,
and league chat. Email addresses and credentials are not intended to be shared with league
members. Administrators and authorized support personnel may access information needed for
their duties. A private league cannot prevent participants from copying content; do not
share invite codes or another person's private information without permission.

We use Supabase for authentication and database services and Vercel for web hosting. Authorized staff review sports data; Authorized staff handle privacy, accessibility and security requests according to their responsibilities. The service operates through the Brock Fantasy association and gameplay websites. Hosting, transactional
email, monitoring, backups, and any native push providers process information needed to
deliver their services. We limit provider access through appropriate contractual and
technical controls and remain accountable for our handling of information.

Information may be processed outside Canada and may be accessible to authorities under those
countries' laws. Contact the privacy contact for information about provider processing locations. We may disclose
information where required or otherwise permitted by applicable law, including to respond to
valid legal process or protect against serious harm. A transfer to a successor operator
would require appropriate safeguards and notice or consent where required.

## Cookies and device storage

We use browser cookies for login, account recovery, request security, and your light/dark
preference. Current expiry periods appear in the
[retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html#browser-and-device-storage).
Clearing or blocking necessary cookies can prevent sign-in; clearing the theme cookie resets
that preference. Supported native clients store session information on the device, which you
can clear through the app/device controls. We do not use these mechanisms for behavioural
advertising.

## Retention, deletion, and protection

Retention periods and targets are described in the
[data retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html). Account deletion removes
the authentication identity and related personal account records on successful completion.
Some league history, chat text, and restricted audit records can remain; removing an account
reference alone does not make them anonymous. See the
[account-deletion policy](account-deletion-notice.html) for residual content,
exceptions, and backup handling.

We use access restrictions, protected communications, authentication controls, and audit
trails appropriate to the information and risk. No system is completely secure. We assess
suspected breaches, take containment measures, and notify affected people and regulators
when applicable law requires.

## Access, correction, and complaints

Contact the privacy contact to request access to your information, an explanation of its
use/disclosure, correction, deletion, or an accessible format. We verify identity
proportionately and protect other people's information. We aim to provide access at no
charge; any permitted fee would be explained in advance.

Where PIPEDA applies, access requests receive a substantive response within 30 calendar days
of receipt. We will explain a permitted extension, refusal, and available complaint rights
as required. The deadline concerns access requests and is not a universal deletion deadline.
See the OPC's
[access-request guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/accessing-personal-information/obligations-for-organizations/02_05_d_54_ati_02/).

You may raise concerns with us or the relevant privacy regulator, including the
[Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/), where applicable.

## Changes

We will publish a revised date and notify users of material changes through the service or
email. We will obtain fresh consent where a new purpose requires it. Contact the privacy
contact with questions.
