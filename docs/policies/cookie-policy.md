# Cookies and device storage

**Last revised:** October 29, 2026\
**Contact:** [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:tymabee@proton.me)

Brock Fantasy uses necessary cookies and device storage to provide account access, protect requests and operate the features you choose. Optional appearance preferences are stored only with your permission. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or visitor profiling.

## Necessary storage

Session cookies support sign-in and renewal. Security cookies protect account requests, while short-lived confirmation and recovery cookies complete authentication flows. Blocking necessary storage can prevent these features from working. Signing out and clearing browser storage remove local session information, subject to the service’s authentication controls.

Unsaved lineup edits may use tab storage so they survive a reload. This storage supports the lineup editor and is separate from optional appearance preferences.

## Optional preferences

You may allow or reject preference storage with equally available controls. Allowing preferences lets the service remember your light or dark theme for up to 180 days. Theme switching remains available without persistent storage. Rejecting preferences does not prevent account access or league participation.

The cookie-choice record lasts up to 180 days and contains no tracking identifier. Use **Cookie settings** in the footer to review or change your choice. Withdrawing permission removes the stored theme preference.

## Email and draft choices

Launch and project-news subscriptions are separate from account registration and cookies. Draft email preferences are also controlled separately. Cookie consent does not subscribe you to marketing, and an email subscription does not grant permission for optional browser storage.

## Retention and questions

The [data retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html#browser-and-device-storage) lists the configured lifetimes and controls for session, security, recovery and preference storage. Renewing a session can set a fresh cookie. Provider records and independently retained device copies follow their own applicable retention rules.

See the [privacy policy](privacy-notice.html) for processing purposes, service providers and your rights. Contact Brock Fantasy support for questions or accessible assistance.
