# Support policy

**Last revised:** October 29, 2026\
**Contact:** [Brock Fantasy support](mailto:bfa.support64@gmail.com)

## Coverage and contact

Support handles account access, email verification and recovery, invitations, drafts and
transactions, accessibility barriers, data/scoring concerns, community reports, and deletion
requests. Contact us by email; use the in-app Report action for individual chat messages.

**Support hours:** Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m., America/Toronto. A business
day means a day within these staffed hours. Statistics staff check data daily, including
weekends; that daily data work does not establish weekend support or continuous monitoring.

## Response targets

| Request                                                                     | First human response target                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ordinary questions, account issues, or accessibility requests               | Within 2 business days.                                                                                  |
| Suspected compromise, serious harassment, or an outage affecting many users | Prioritized during staffed hours; aim for review within 1 business day.                                  |
| Data/scoring issue affecting a current draft or matchup                     | Aim for initial review within 1 business day; verified source information may take longer.               |
| Moderation appeal                                                           | Acknowledge within 2 business days; aim for a decision within 10 business days.                          |
| Manual account deletion                                                     | Acknowledge within 2 business days; aim to complete within 30 calendar days after identity verification. |

These are service targets, not guaranteed resolution deadlines or a service-level agreement.
We will give a status update if investigation takes longer. Statutory privacy deadlines
apply independently; where PIPEDA applies, access requests require a substantive response
within 30 calendar days of receipt, subject to permitted extensions.

## What to include

Send your account email, relevant league/game/player identifier, the time and time zone, a
short description, and the expected result. For data issues, include an official source link
and the disputed statistic. Redact other people's information from screenshots.

Never send passwords, authenticator secrets, session cookies, recovery codes, or sign-in
links. Use the approved password-recovery process. We may verify identity or league
authority before making a change or discussing private records; we will request only what is
necessary.

## Resolution and escalation

Support may troubleshoot, explain rules, refer evidence to data reviewers, or escalate a
case. Score changes require authorized source review and an audit reason; support does not
award discretionary points or undo deadlines simply because a request was made.

Requests remain open until an administrator closes them. If unresolved, reply to the same
correspondence and ask for review. An authorized reviewer who did not approve the original decision reviews the appeal. Privacy complaints go to the privacy contact; security incidents are escalated to the authorized security-incident owner. Moderation appeals follow the
[moderation policy](community-moderation-guidelines.html).

We will assess accessibility barriers and provide a usable alternative or a progress update
where feasible. You need not disclose a medical diagnosis to report an accessibility issue.

Support correspondence is used for handling the request and retained under the
[retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.html); it is not consent for
marketing. For imminent danger, contact local emergency services. Brock Fantasy support is
not an emergency-response service.
