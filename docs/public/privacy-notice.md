## Scope and purposes

Brock Fantasy is a free fantasy-sports service for private leagues involving Brock varsity athletics. This policy covers account holders, visitors, support correspondents, and athlete information used in the service. The named operator is responsible for the personal information it handles.

We use information to authenticate users, operate leagues and league chat, calculate and explain fantasy results, send service communications, respond to requests, investigate abuse, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information, display advertising, or use advertising trackers.

## Information we handle

| Category                           | Examples and purpose                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account and authentication         | Email, display name, account ID, verification/recovery status, password and optional authenticator information. Supabase Auth processes credentials; passwords are not stored in readable form by Brock Fantasy. |
| League activity                    | Memberships, team names, invitations, draft picks and queues, lineups, trades, waivers, results, and related timestamps, to run and audit competition.                                                           |
| Community information              | Chat text, reports, mute settings, and moderation decisions, to provide communication and enforce community rules.                                                                                               |
| Technical and security information | IP address or related rate-limit signals, request/device/browser metadata where logged by our providers, session/security cookies, and audit records, to secure and troubleshoot the service.                    |
| Notifications                      | In-app notices and delivery records; device push tokens and message payloads if you enable a supported native notification feature. Native push is outside the initial web-beta release.                         |
| Support and privacy requests       | Contact details, correspondence, evidence you submit, and necessary verification information, to handle the request.                                                                                             |
| Athlete and sports information     | Athlete names, team/position, jersey numbers, approved roster details, schedules, results, historical statistics, and source revisions, to present player information and calculate fantasy scoring.             |

We do not request student numbers, payment details, dates of birth, phone numbers, or precise device location. An optional avatar URL is supported in the data model; if enabled, it may reveal profile information and cause requests to the image host. Avoid putting sensitive information in chat, team names, or support attachments.

Athlete information can be personal information even when obtained from a public source. Source permission and appropriate collection/use remain necessary. Athletes may contact the privacy contact about accuracy or use of their information without creating an account.

## Cookies and device storage

We use browser cookies for login, account recovery, request security, and your light/dark preference. Current expiry periods appear in the [retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.md#browser-and-device-storage). Clearing or blocking necessary cookies can prevent sign-in; clearing the theme cookie resets that preference. Supported native clients store session information on the device, which you can clear through the app/device controls. We do not use these mechanisms for behavioural advertising.

## Retention, deletion, and protection

Our proposed periods are in the [data retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.md). Account deletion removes the authentication identity and related personal account records on successful completion. Some league history, chat text, and restricted audit records can remain; removing an account reference alone does not make them anonymous. See the [account-deletion policy](account-deletion-notice.md) for residual content, exceptions, and backup handling.

We use access restrictions, protected communications, authentication controls, and audit trails appropriate to the information and risk. No system is completely secure. We assess suspected breaches, take containment measures, and notify affected people and regulators when applicable law requires.

## Access, correction, and complaints

Contact the privacy contact to request access to your information, an explanation of its use/disclosure, correction, deletion, or an accessible format. We verify identity proportionately and protect other people's information. We aim to provide access at no charge; any permitted fee would be explained in advance.

Where PIPEDA applies, access requests receive a substantive response within 30 calendar days of receipt. We will explain a permitted extension, refusal, and available complaint rights as required. The deadline concerns access requests and is not a universal deletion deadline. See the OPC's [access-request guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/accessing-personal-information/obligations-for-organizations/02_05_d_54_ati_02/).

You may raise concerns with us or the relevant privacy regulator, including the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/), where applicable.

## Changes

We will publish a revised date and notify users of material changes through the service or email. We will obtain fresh consent where a new purpose requires it. Contact the privacy contact with questions.
