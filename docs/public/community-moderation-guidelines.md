## Scope and expected conduct

This policy applies to league chat, account and team names, invitations, reports, and conduct through Brock Fantasy. Compete fairly, respect other participants and athletes, and keep disagreement focused on ideas, rules, and evidence. Criticism of a score, administrator decision, or sporting performance is allowed when it follows these rules.

Do not:

- Threaten, harass, stalk, bully, or direct hateful or discriminatory abuse at a person or group.
- Publish private identifying information, credentials, non-consensual intimate material, sexual exploitation, or other unlawful content.
- Impersonate others, spam, solicit, advertise, send malicious links, or promote betting or paid contests.
- Cheat, collude to manipulate competition, exploit security weaknesses, evade restrictions, or knowingly falsify sports data.
- Retaliate against someone for a report or appeal, or deliberately submit false or coordinated abusive reports.

## Reporting and personal controls

Use **Report** beside a chat message. For patterns of behaviour, name/invitation issues, or extra context, email support with the league, relevant message identifiers, times, and supporting details. The current in-app action sends a basic report; email support when a fuller explanation is needed.

**Mute** hides that member's chat from your view in the relevant league. It does not remove the member, delete messages, or prevent reports and administrative review. Reporting does not automatically prove a violation or guarantee immediate removal.

Avoid circulating harmful content beyond what is necessary to report it. We limit disclosure of a reporter's identity, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality where context or a lawful process reveals it.

## Review and enforcement

Authorized platform administrators review reports. League commissioners may hide or restore messages only where the service grants that permission; platform-wide restrictions remain an operator responsibility. Moderators consider context, seriousness, repetition, likely harm, and evidence. Disagreement or criticism alone is not grounds for a sanction.

Possible measures include a warning, hiding content, a feature restriction, suspension, or termination. Serious threats, exploitation, or urgent security risks may justify immediate action without a prior warning. Any correction of competition activity must also follow league rules and the audited scoring/transaction process.

We record material decisions and, where safe and lawful, tell the affected person the rule, reason, scope/duration, and appeal route. A moderator with a personal conflict should refer the matter to another authorized reviewer where possible. Hidden messages may be retained for review; hiding is not deletion.

## Appeals

Email support within 30 calendar days of notice, with the decision reference, your explanation, and any new evidence. We may consider later appeals where justified. The proposed target is acknowledgment within 2 business days and a decision within 10 business days; we will explain delays.

An appeal is reviewed by someone other than the original decision-maker where staffing permits. We may uphold, change, or reverse the decision and communicate the outcome. Filing an appeal does not automatically lift a protective restriction or limit any legal complaint rights.

## Safety, records, and changes

Support is not continuously monitored. For imminent danger, contact local emergency services. We may preserve necessary evidence or disclose it to appropriate authorities where lawful.

Report, appeal, and enforcement records follow the [privacy policy](privacy-notice.md) and [retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.md). We will publish an updated effective date and communicate material policy changes.
