## Requesting deletion

Sign in, open **Account**, select **Delete account**, and type `DELETE` to confirm. Submit only when you are ready: successful deletion is permanent and has no built-in cancellation or recovery period.

If you cannot sign in or the request fails, email the contact above from your registered address where possible. We will verify control of the account proportionately; knowing a display name or league invite code is not sufficient. Do not email passwords, recovery codes, or login links.

## Effects of successful deletion

Successful deletion removes your authentication account and profile, memberships and platform-role assignments, mute settings, in-app notifications, registered device push tokens and associated delivery records, and account-linked command-retry records. Reports you filed are also deleted by the current account cascade; necessary incident evidence may be retained separately under a documented exception.

You lose access to leagues and teams associated with that account. Historical fantasy teams, drafts, roster transactions, and scoring results can remain with account ownership references cleared. Deletion does not reverse completed picks, trades, or scores, and does not delete an entire league used by other people. Contact support first if commissioner responsibilities need a handover.

Creating a new account later does not restore the deleted identity, previous memberships, or authority over retained teams. Account deletion is different from signing out, uninstalling the app, leaving a league, or disabling notifications.

## Residual information

The current deletion flow does not automatically erase every chat body, fantasy-team name, audit entity identifier, free-text reason, or historical payload. These may still identify you after an author/owner reference is cleared. Such records remain subject to the privacy policy; we do not describe them as anonymous merely because a reference was removed.

You can ask for residual personal content to be reviewed. Our proposed target is to remove, redact, or appropriately de-identify unnecessary residual information within 30 calendar days after verification. We may retain a limited record needed for a live dispute, security/moderation investigation, access complaint, legal obligation, or accurate competition history. We will explain applicable exceptions where lawful and use the periods in the [retention schedule](data-retention-schedule.md).

Official athlete records, game results, and sports statistics are separate from your fantasy-manager account and are not deleted with it. Athletes may contact the privacy contact about their information.

## Timing, failures, and confirmation

The in-app request attempts live-account deletion immediately. Treat it as complete only after the service reports success. An error means deletion may not have completed; contact support rather than assuming that signing out removed the account. Some administrator accounts can require additional handling because retained imports or operational records reference them.

For verified manual requests, the proposed completion target is 30 calendar days after verification. We will confirm completion or explain remaining work, retained categories, and any lawful limitation. This target is an operator proposal, not a statutory deadline for every deletion request.

We aim to revoke account access when deletion completes. Previously issued credentials and cached screens may take time to expire; successful deletion should not be interpreted as immediate removal of every local copy. Sign out and clear app/browser storage on shared devices.

## Backups and third-party copies

Deleted information may remain in restricted backup copies until they expire. The proposed maximum backup window is 90 calendar days after live-system removal; the actual provider configuration must be confirmed before publication. Backups are used for recovery, and deletion instructions must be reapplied before restored data is returned to service.

We direct processors to handle deletion under their contracts and applicable law. We cannot remove screenshots or copies independently kept by other league members, or information a provider must retain under its own legal obligations. Contact the privacy contact if you want access to your information before deleting the account.
